PhishLens

Checking the analyser...

When you open an email in Gmail, PhishLens shows a banner above it saying whether it looks like phishing, and why.

  • Red: likely phishing
  • Amber: signs of phishing, check carefully
  • Green: no signs found
  • Grey: not checked, or only partly

Emails are analysed inside your browser. Their content is never sent anywhere, never stored, and PhishLens has no servers, analytics or tracking.
