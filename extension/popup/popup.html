PhishLens

Checking the local server...

To start it, run this from the phishlens folder:

.venv\Scripts\python -m uvicorn api.main:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000

PhishLens checks the emails you open in Gmail for signs of phishing and explains why. The analysis runs on a small server on your own computer, so email content never leaves it.
