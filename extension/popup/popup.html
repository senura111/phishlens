PhishLens

Checking the local server...

To start it, run this from the phishlens folder:

.venv\Scripts\python -m uvicorn api.main:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000

When you open an email in Gmail, PhishLens shows a banner above it saying whether it looks like phishing, and why.

  • Red: likely phishing
  • Amber: signs of phishing, check carefully
  • Green: no signs found
  • Grey: not checked, or only partly

The analysis runs on a small server on your own computer, so email content never leaves it. No email is stored, and nothing is sent anywhere else.
